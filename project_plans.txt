Chapter 1 – Introduction
------------------------
1.1 Background
   - Mental health services are cost-prohibitive or inaccessible in many regions.
1.2 Problem Statement
   - Lack of anonymous, immediate emotional support.
1.3 Objectives & Scope
   - Build 24/7 AI chatbot for guided conversation.
   - Exclusions: no clinical diagnosis or emergency intervention.

Chapter 2 – Literature Review
-----------------------------
2.1 AI Chatbots in Therapy
   - Survey of Woebot, Wysa, and academic studies on efficacy.
2.2 LLM Cost & Performance
   - GPT-3.5 Turbo vs GPT-4 vs open-source LLaMA2 benchmarks.
2.3 Privacy & Security Standards
   - GDPR, HIPAA considerations for data handling.

Chapter 4 – Project Planning & Milestones
-----------------------------------------
4.1 Timeline & Phases
   - Phase 1 (Wk1-2): Stakeholder interviews, SRS drafting.
   - Phase 2 (Wk3-4): UI/UX wireframes, tech stack validation.
   - Phase 3 (Wk5-8): Core feature development (chat UI, message API).
   - Phase 4 (Wk9-10): AI integration, encryption, end-to-end testing.
   - Phase 5 (Wk11-12): Load testing, bug fixes, documentation, demo prep.
4.2 Milestones & Deliverables
   - M1 (End Wk2): Approved SRS & architecture.
   - M2 (End Wk4): UI prototype with mock messaging.
   - M3 (Mid Wk6): Backend API & session management ready.
   - M4 (End Wk8): Live AI responses in UI; test logs.
   - M5 (End Wk12): Final demo, user feedback report, submission.

Chapter 5 – Risk Management & Quality Assurance
